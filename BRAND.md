# Basil — Brand Voice

> **Read this before writing any user-facing copy.** Headlines, descriptions, empty
> states, error messages, onboarding text — all of it should sound like Basil.

---

## Voice

Basil sounds like a thoughtful friend who happens to be good with money — not a
financial advisor, not a startup pitch. The tone is warm, direct, and quietly
confident: it explains what it does without overselling. Sentences are short.
Language is plain. We say "your money" not "your financial journey."

**Basil sounds like:** a handwritten note, a kitchen conversation, a well-organized notebook.

**Basil does not sound like:** a bank, a VC deck, a productivity guru, or a fintech landing page.

---

## Principles

- **Calm confidence.** State what the app does. Don't hype it.
- **Plain language.** If a simpler word exists, use it.
- **Show, don't sell.** Describe what happens, not why it's amazing.
- **Respect the user.** They're not broken. They don't need fixing. They want clarity.
- **Be honest about scope.** It's a budgeting app. Say that.

---

## Tone examples

| Instead of | Write |
|------------|-------|
| "Take control of your finances" | "Know where your money goes" |
| "Unlock powerful insights" | "See your spending over time" |
| "Smart categorization powered by AI" | "Categorize once, done forever" |
| "Get started on your financial journey" | "Sign in to get started" |
| "We've got you covered!" | (just explain the feature) |

---

## What NOT to say

| Cliché | Why |
|--------|-----|
| "Take control of your finances" | Implies the user is out of control. Patronizing. |
| "Your financial journey" | Nobody calls it a journey. They call it paying rent. |
| "Powered by AI / intelligent automation" | It's pattern matching and rules. Be honest. |
| "Unlock insights" | Vague, corporate, means nothing. |
| "Crush your goals" | Wrong energy. Basil is calm, not a gym bro. |
| "All-in-one financial platform" | It's a budgeting app. |
| "Smart money moves" | Basil doesn't tell you what to do. It shows you what happened. |
| "Synergy / leverage / optimize" | Consultant-speak. |
| "Free forever" | Don't promise pricing that hasn't been decided. |
| "Join thousands of users" | Don't use social proof you don't have. |

---

## Rules

- No exclamation marks in headlines. Calm confidence, not enthusiasm.
- Don't explain Plaid to the user. They don't care how the plumbing works.
- Don't say "simple" — let the simplicity speak for itself.
- Avoid "just" as a minimizer ("just connect your bank"). It reads as dismissive.
- No emoji in copy. The design system carries the warmth; the words stay clean.
- Error messages should be helpful, not apologetic. "Couldn't sync — try again" not
  "Oops! Something went wrong! We're so sorry!"

---

## Key copy (reference)

**Tagline:** Know where your money goes.

**Description:** Basil connects to your bank, sorts your transactions, and learns
how you think about spending — so you don't have to do the same work twice.

**Value props:**
- **Categorize once, done forever.** Move a transaction to a category and Basil
  remembers. Every similar transaction — past and future — follows suit.
- **See the month as it unfolds.** Track spending by category, spot what's left,
  and catch surprises before they compound.
- **Months of context, not just a snapshot.** Spending patterns, cash flow, savings
  rate — watch the shape of your finances over time.
- **Your data stays yours.** No third-party cloud. No data selling. Your financial
  life lives on infrastructure you can trust.
